Approval Route

The probation UAT workflow uses a fixed approval route so every contract is reviewed consistently.
  1. Incumbent captures and submits the KPI contract.
  2. Assigned supervisor reviews the KPI contract.
  3. HR approves the KPI contract.
  4. Incumbent captures KPI progress and self-assessment marks.
  5. Assigned supervisor captures supervisor marks and personal dimensions.
  6. HR reviews the calculated result and approves the final evaluation.
